ClipFarmer

Turns long livestream and VOD footage into captioned vertical clips, queued for review before anything is published.

ClipFarmer is a personal tool. It helps the person who runs it cut clips from footage they have permission to use, add captions and attribution, review each clip, and publish it to the social accounts they manage: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels.

Nothing is published automatically to an account you have not connected yourself, and clips are held for review first.
