Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what information ClipFarmer handles when you connect an account and use it to publish clips. ClipFarmer is a personal, self-hosted tool. It is run by its developer ("we") for the accounts the developer owns or manages.

What we access

When you connect a TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or YouTube account, the platform gives ClipFarmer an access token for the permissions you approve. For TikTok this is basic profile information and permission to publish video. For Meta it is permission to read the Pages and Instagram accounts you select and to publish to them. ClipFarmer uses these only to show which account is connected and to publish clips you have approved.

What we store

  • Access and refresh tokens for connected accounts, stored on the machine that runs ClipFarmer.
  • Source footage and the clips, captions and transcripts made from it.
  • A record of each publication, including the platform and the post identifier, so posts can be tracked and removed.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it with third parties, except the platform you publish to. We do not use TikTok or Meta data to build profiles or for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Raw footage is kept for a short period and then deleted. Finished clips and publication records are kept until you remove them. You can disconnect an account at any time, which removes its stored tokens, or ask us to delete a clip or post. Where the platform allows it, removing a clip also deletes the post on that platform.

Your choices

You can revoke ClipFarmer's access from your account settings on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or YouTube. Revoking access stops further publishing. To ask for deletion of your data, contact us using the address below.
